THE ARCHITECTURAL CONCEPT

Raw Stone.
Refined Street Spice.

Oley & Thusoo was conceived as an architectural counterpoint to conventional dining in Islamabad. Situated along Faisal Ave, our warm stone facade and open structural geometry create a calm, tactile sanctuary for casual street food lovers.

We take street culinary staples—crisp handcrafted gol gappay with spiced mint infusions, tang-laden papri chaat—and elevate them alongside flame-kissed charcoal kebabs and glazed wings. No pretense, just deliberate flavors and honest design.
